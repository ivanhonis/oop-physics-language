# II/14. The extension staircase — yes, with a readout limit

**The question.** The target proof of [III/2, 8.](../../III-frontier/III-02-open-questions_en.md) after II/13: at an identical number of contracts per site (six), does extension step with the density — sparsely the line, in the middle the plane, at high density space? The proof was produced with the package method ([Appendix D](../../appendix/D-package-method_en.md)); *before* the rulebook was fixed, an error also had to be corrected: III/2, 8. had recorded the ball law of space as linear ($+{\sim}6r$), whereas it is quadratic ($4r^2+2$) — the correction was made before the protocol was built, and stated ([PKG-14-1](PKG-14-1-rulebook_en.md)).

**The system and the contract.** 512 sites; 1536 units of smoothness contract, exactly six per site; every candidate is a weave network, and the side length of every wrapped direction is at least 8 (the legacy of the resonance diagnosis of II/13). Candidates: **line** (a 512-ring with 1-2-3 step wiring), **plane** (a 16×32 triangular weave), **space** (an 8×8×8 cubic weave); control is the 8×64 stretched plane; obligatory scan: all 220 triple wirings of the line family up to step 12 (a fixed, stated bound).

**The ladders.** The ladder of all 224 networks by two independent routes (the eigenproblem of the machinery and the closed Fourier form of the weaves), agreeing to within $7.9\cdot10^{-14}$; the trace sum is exactly 3072 everywhere. Here the distinctness of the candidates is not an assumption but a proof: space is a **bipartite network** — its ladder is exactly symmetric about 6 ($10^{-15}$), that of the line and the plane is not — the coincidence trap of II/13 does not apply. The scan caught something twice: 20 disconnected weaves live in the family (all of their steps even — the rulebook's derivation that "every entrant is connected" was mistaken, the correction stands in [PKG-14-2](PKG-14-2-ladders_en.md)), and the 220 members fall into 216 ladder classes (the four coincidences are step relabelings, not degeneracy).

**The result — the race** (after independent recomputation, [PKG-14-3](PKG-14-3-race_en.md)):

| N instances | 1 | 2–129 | 130–255 | 256–511 | 512 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| winner among the three | all (0) | **line** | **plane** | **space** | all (3072) |

**According to the fixed two-band rule the verdict is: yes — both rungs of the staircase hold, connectedly.** The extension number steps with the density. And the structure of the decisive boundary can be stated: **space takes over the race exactly at half filling** (N = 256) — not at a degree boundary, but in the *middle* of its 68-beat-wide central shelf. The mechanism is the **bipartite mark**: in the upper hemisphere the winner is the one whose ladder is symmetric and widest — the mirror pushes the unfilled beats highest. The candidate shell-fit rule is to be refined accordingly: on a small system (II/13) and in the lower comb the degree filling decides, while the plane–space boundary is decided by the mirror ([III/1, 6–7.](../../III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)). *Full field, honestly:* the declared weaves are nowhere strict winners of the field — at every filling a tuned member of the line family is the cheapest, and the disadvantage of space is 0.3–2.2% throughout; at low filling the line alternates with its own disconnected multiplications in a regular comb, and the full end is taken by the all-odd-step lines (themselves carrying the bipartite mark). **The advantage of extension is real, but thin, and its carrier is the mirror symmetry — which can be produced on a line as well.**

**The result — the readout** ([PKG-14-4](PKG-14-4-readout_en.md)). On the winner (space, N = 290, a closed degree) the rebuilder found all 1536 real contracts, with none missing — but the fixed jump rule also drew in the antipodal mirror point: 256 phantom edges (a complete antipodal pairing), and the measured ball is 1, 8, 32, 88 instead of the recorded 1, 7, 25, 63. **The closing of the loop failed partially at this size** — not destructively, and in a way different from II/13: there is no exact coincidence (the sentinel did not even signal — the form of "exact agreement" proved too narrow), the echo won the jump race; alongside this a protocol gap was recorded (signed versus absolute-value jump). The diagnosis (outside the scope, as a candidate): on 12×12×12 the jump singles out six sharply (8.9-fold), the rebuilding is 5184/5184 with no phantom, and the ball increment is 6, 18, 38, 66, 102 — **exactly $4r^2+2$, the measured growth law of three extensions, up to $r=5$**; the echo dies away with size. The mechanism, stated: the antipodal echo is the mirror pairing of the bipartite mark — **the same symmetry that won the race for space spoils its readout at small size.** The diagnosis has since been confirmed by a repetition with a fixed scope: on the 12-weave all four conditions fixed in advance hold, and the echo falls to 16% of the neighbor closeness — the size artifact goes from candidate to theorem ([PKG-14-5](PKG-14-5-readout-repeat_en.md)).

**Checking against reality.** An internal race, without a direct measured target; it has two measured echoes: the standard phenomenon of density-/pressure-driven structural changes (here in the smallest, exact staircase form), and the particle-hole symmetry at half filling on bipartite lattices (a standard theorem) — the measured relative of the mirror mechanism ([Appendix C](../../appendix/C-benchmarks_en.md)).

**Import ledger:** zero new item; the obligation to scan caught something (disconnected family members — a derivation error of the rulebook corrected), and the two rule-lessons of the protocol are recorded. **What it confirmed:** a complete staircase verdict — extension is density-dependent, and steps with the density; the mirror rule is a new candidate ([III/1, 7.](../../III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)); the scope of shell fit is refined; the quadratic reading of space is measured exactly on a large weave (as a theorem after the fixed-scope repetition — [PKG-14-5](PKG-14-5-readout-repeat_en.md)); and the root signal of the field finding: because of the thin advantage and the tunable line imitation, selection cannot be decided on a free network — it can be decided where the contracts are born ([III/2, 7.](../../III-frontier/III-02-open-questions_en.md)). Direction of extension: the next rung (space versus four extensions, coordination eight, on a sufficiently large weave) — the fixed-scope repetition of the readout on the 12-weave is complete and stands ([PKG-14-5](PKG-14-5-readout-repeat_en.md)).

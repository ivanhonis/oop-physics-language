# The history of the versions

The language was built layer by layer; this file records the turning points, from the most recent backwards. (In the unified releases this was Appendix A — its content moved here and was retired from the appendices.)

## [Unreleased]

- The document moved to a **modular structure**: a separate file per chapter, per proof and per package, with stable identifiers, prepared for a bilingual mirror structure. The content is unchanged — it matches the unified release 1.3.

## [1.3]

- Introduced the **package method**: larger derivations are produced in individually checked derivation packages with a rulebook fixed in advance (trail: Appendix D).
- Ran the **extension race** ([II/13](II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/proof_en.md)): a partial yes for extension — at high density the plane beats the line and the segmented one; the only strict victory of the plane is its own closed degree.
- New candidate: the **shell-fit rule** (III/1, 6.); derived theorem: the weave-coincidence lemma.
- Recorded the small-size limit of the readout (wrap-around resonance) and the size diagnosis (+4r ball law).
- III/2, 8. (the extension number) was reformulated into extension-staircase and shell-fit form.

## [1.2]

- Ran the **network race** ([II/12](II-proofs/II-12-network-race/proof_en.md)): the failure of the selection candidate; two new theorems: the **collapse theorem** and the **trace tie**.
- New candidate import: the **equal rank of the sites** (III/1, 5.).
- III/2, 2. (the origin of locality) reformulated; root signal: in the language the contract is a free resource.

## [1.1]

- Recorded the **locality-readout proof** ([II/11](II-proofs/II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md)): adjacency is a view of the finished state; the surface signature; the extension number is measurable.
- New candidate theorem: **the output is not data** (a scope-bound view fact; III/1, 4.).
- New open question: **the object born from a pattern** (III/2, 7.).

## [0.7–0.10]

- The **law of identity** and the magic numbers (II/7); the exclusion–repulsion race (II/8); the Kagome proof (II/9); the internal witness (II/10).

## [0.5–0.6]

- The **uniformity proof** closed (II/6).

## [0.4]

- Introduced the **import ledger**; the triangle prediction stated in advance (II/5).

## [0.3]

- Set the target proof of quantization (the spectrum of hydrogen, II/4); the engine law stated, still unconfirmed.

## [0.2]

- Measurement on the pair was still treated as a conjecture; the name "mixture" for the state weighting was replaced (in physics "mixed state" is the taken name of the view).

# C) Measurements and published benchmarks

- **Petta et al. (2005):** exchange oscillation in a double quantum dot — [II/2](../II-proofs/II-02-exchange-oscillation/proof_en.md).
- **Tarucha et al. (1996):** the filling peaks of an artificial atom (2, 6, 12; secondary peaks 4, 9) — [II/7](../II-proofs/II-07-bowl-magic-numbers/proof_en.md), [II/8](../II-proofs/II-08-exclusion-vs-repulsion/proof_en.md).
- **Yan–Huse–White (2011):** Kagome infinite-lattice numerics — [II/9](../II-proofs/II-09-kagome/proof_en.md).
- **Singh–Huse (2007):** the structured solid candidate — [II/9](../II-proofs/II-09-kagome/proof_en.md).
- **Waldtmann et al. (1998), Läuchli et al. (2011, 2016):** exact computations of Kagome pieces, singlet mass — [II/9](../II-proofs/II-09-kagome/proof_en.md).
- **Han et al. (2012):** herbertsmithite, the continuous spectrum of fractionalized excitations — [II/9](../II-proofs/II-09-kagome/proof_en.md).
- **Pal–Huse (2010), Luitz et al. (2015):** the numerics of the localization tip-over — [II/10](../II-proofs/II-10-internal-witness/proof_en.md).
- **Schreiber et al. (2015), Smith et al. (2016):** memory persistence on cold atoms and trapped ions — [II/10](../II-proofs/II-10-internal-witness/proof_en.md).
- **Atas et al. (2013):** the constants of the level-spacing ratio — [II/10](../II-proofs/II-10-internal-witness/proof_en.md).
- **Renou et al. (2021, experiments 2022):** the sharp exclusion of real weights — [III/2, question 1](../III-frontier/III-02-open-questions_en.md).
- **The Bonner–Fisher line, standard exact small-ring numerics:** the ground energy of the 12-ring — [II/11](../II-proofs/II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md).
- **Eisert, Cramer and Plenio (2010):** the review of the surface law — [II/11](../II-proofs/II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md).
- **Van Raamsdonk (2010):** space built from entanglement — [II/11](../II-proofs/II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md).
- **The Peierls distortion** (the measured alternating bond length of polyacetylene; standard measured data): the distortion of the half-filled chain into a pairwise pattern — the measured relative of the tendency to dust of [II/12](../II-proofs/II-12-network-race/proof_en.md).
- **Shell-closure stability** (Tarucha et al., 1996 — the anchor of II/7) **and the density-/pressure-driven structural changes** as a standard measured class of phenomena: the measured echoes of [II/13](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/proof_en.md) and [II/14](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/proof_en.md).
- **The body-centered (BCC) lattice — the structure of iron, among others** (standard measured data): the real counterpart of the coordination-eight space candidate of [II/15](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/proof_en.md).
- **Particle-hole symmetry at half filling on bipartite lattices** (a standard theorem): the measured relative of the mirror mechanism of [II/14](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/proof_en.md).
- **Frauchiger and Renner (2018), Proietti et al. (2019), Bong et al. (2020):** the no-go theorem of nested witnesses and its photonic experiments — [III/1, 4.](../III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)
- **The spectrum of hydrogen, the ladders of quantum wells, the universality of crystal bands and the level pattern of triangular molecular magnets:** standard measured data — [II/3](../II-proofs/II-03-box/proof_en.md)–[II/6](../II-proofs/II-06-universality/proof_en.md).

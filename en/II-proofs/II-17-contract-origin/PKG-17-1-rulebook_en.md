# PKG-17-1 — The origin of the contract: the stability of the flat state (derivation package)

**It contains no computation** — it fixes the rules before every computation; it cannot be modified afterwards.

## 1. Question (one sentence, fixed in advance)

If the content of the contract is not given from outside but generated by the finished state of the instances — does the system return to the perfectly flat contract network, does it crumble into pairs, or does something remain between the two?

**What this package does not ask:** how many extensions the resulting network has, how large the coordination number is, and what the form of attraction is. These are the subject of later packages; here a single yes-or-no question stands, deliberately.

## 2. Inputs

The complete machinery and **computed control** of [II/11](../II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md): the pairwise closeness measure from the finished state, the exact projector of the degenerate zero-cost subspace, the blind rebuilder and the ball reading. The bond contract of [II/1](../II-01-pair-bond/proof_en.md) in closed form; the frustration remainder of [II/5](../II-05-triangle/proof_en.md); the collapse theorem of [II/12](../II-12-network-race/proof_en.md) and the pairwise covering decided by hand on the bond side; [exclusive ownership](../../I-language/I-07-measurement_en.md) and the [5th law](../../I-language/I-06-identity_en.md).

The three correction lessons of II/15–II/16 are inherited as rules where applicable: the primary computation route is extended-precision (H3); the uniqueness of the elements is verified by machine assertion (H1).

## 3. The fixed system

- **Twelve objects, a contract between all 66 pairs** — the system is identical to the control of [II/11](../II-11-locality-readout/proof_en.md), with the one difference that the **content** of the contracts is not uniform but variable.
- The form of the contract is the vetted bond contract, with pairwise content: $w_{ij} \ge 0$ multiplies the closed form of [II/1](../II-01-pair-bond/proof_en.md). A pair without content (empty) is permitted — $w_{ij} = 0$.
- The state is the **exact minimum of the full state space** (Appendix B, tool 4; 4096 dimensions, exactly by the dense route). In case of degeneracy, the even mixture of the zero-cost subspace, with an **exact projector** — the control method of II/11, unchanged.
- The primary route is extended-precision; the identity tolerance is $10^{-10}$, and the stopping tolerance of the loop is $10^{-9}$ on the content vector.

## 4. The loop and the generating rule

One round of the loop, fixed:

1. from a given content vector ($w$, 66 numbers) the finished state,
2. from the finished state the pairwise closeness ($I_{ij}$) — the measure of II/11, unchanged,
3. from the closeness the new content: $w_{ij} = f(I_{ij})$, then normalization,
4. repetition until stopping or the fixed round count (**200 rounds**).

**The family of the generating rule** (all six members must run):

| Member | $f$ | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | $x$ | direct proportion |
| F2 | $x^2$ | strengthened feedback |
| F3 | $\sqrt{x}$ | damped feedback |
| F4 | $x/(1+x)$ | saturating |
| F5 | $\max(0,\, x - \bar{x})$ | the part above the mean (thresholded) |
| F6 | $x$, but stepping halfway each round | slowed loop (to filter out cycling) |

**Normalization — two forms, both obligatory:** (N1) the sum of the contents is 66 units; (N2) the largest content is 1. Normalization is the place where the budget convention of II/12 could seep back in; it therefore runs in two mutually unrelated forms, and the verdict stands only on the **agreement of the two**.

**Start — four starting points, each with every family member and both normalizations:**

| Start | Description | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | flat + drawn noise, $10^{-3}$ | is the flat state stable |
| I2 | flat + a single pair raised | does the pair corner attract |
| I3 | flat + one matching (6 disjoint pairs) raised | the pairwise covering of II/12 as a basin |
| I4 | flat + the edges of the 12-ring raised | geometry as a basin |

The random seed everywhere: **1166** (= 66 · 17 + 44; a stated, fixed number). The magnitude of the noise and the degree of the raising ($1.1\times$) are fixed.

## 5. Derived preliminary facts

**(T1) The flat state is a fixed point — with one exception.** On a complete network with uniform content every pair of the system is of equal rank; the closeness computed from the finished state is therefore identical on every pair, hence the new content is uniform as well. This is not an expectation but a consequence of symmetry — the question is therefore **not** whether a flat fixed point exists, but whether it is **stable**.

**The exception is F5** (the thresholded member): at the flat point every closeness equals its own mean, so the part above the mean is exactly zero everywhere. From the flat start F5 leads **in a single round to the EMPTY state** — not a fixed point but immediate disintegration. Thresholded generation is therefore symmetry-breaking in itself; this is the substantive content of F5 in the family, and this is why it stays in.

**(T2) The price of the empty contract has no effect in this package.** With obligatory contracts the number of pairs is fixed (66), and continuous generation in general position never gives an exact zero; the price of the empty is thus the same constant for every arrangement, and cannot break the symmetry. **Stated: the price of the empty is therefore not in this package** — its place is the question of the coordination number, where the active/empty choice of the pairs is itself the stake; the name of the continuation is reserved as **PKG-17-4**. This order is not convenience: if the flat state is stable, then there is no distance for the price of the empty to price.

**The one exception here too is F5:** with it the empty contract genuinely appears — and does so immediately and everywhere (T1). F5 is therefore the bridge to the question of the price: it shows that without a price the empty case does not bring equilibrium but carries off the whole network. The behavior of F5 must be **reported separately** in every run.

**(T3) Two known corners, both with computed numbers.** The flat corner is the control of II/11: bond price exactly $45/66 = 0.6818$, degeneracy 132, all 66 closenesses 0.012, spread $10^{-16}$. The pair corner is the bond-side winner of II/12: six disjoint pairs, **zero cost**. The pair corner is therefore unbeatable in cost — the loop does not minimize cost, but the protocol must see this attraction.

**(T4) Degeneracy is the sensitive point of the loop.** The ground state of the flat state is 132-fold degenerate; the first step of the loop is therefore meaningful only with the exact projector. If the noise lifts the degeneracy, the unambiguous ground state is to be used; if it does not, the projector. The switching between the two must be reported round by round — **the number of switches is part of the protocol**, because a jumping loop is not the same as a smooth one.

**(T5) The loop contains two opposing forces.** Generation is self-reinforcing (the closer pair receives a stronger contract, which brings it closer still); [frustration](../II-05-triangle/proof_en.md) works against it (a member cannot be part of two full bonds). The outcome is the race of these two. Which one wins is a **registered question, not a prediction**.

## 6. Pre-registered facts and questions

**From theorems:** the flat start without noise stays exactly in place with every family member and both normalizations (T1) — this is the **obligatory zero test** of the loop, before the run. Reproducing the control numbers of II/11 (0.6818; 132; spread $10^{-16}$) and the numbers of the 12-ring (bond price 0.3011; closenesses 0.444 / 0.123 / 0.066 / 0.045 / 0.037) is a **B3 precondition**: until they agree, the loop does not start.

**A prediction: deliberately none.** Registered questions:

- **(Q-a, the main question of the proof)** is the flat fixed point stable, and if not, in which direction does it run away;
- **(Q-b)** does the final state depend on the start (I1–I4) — is there one basin or several;
- **(Q-c)** do the six family members and the two normalizations give the same verdict;
- **(Q-d)** in the intermediate case, how graded is the content vector (how many distinct levels), and does the closeness list jump;
- **(Q-e)** on reaching the pair corner: is it an exact matching, or only a partial one.

## 7. Verdict rule (fixed)

The final state is to be classified into **one of four classes**, separately for every start–family-member–normalization triple:

- **EMPTY** — every content is exactly zero; the network has ceased. Normalization is then not to be performed; the class itself is the result. (It takes priority: if this holds, there is no other classification.)
- **FLAT** — the spread of the contents at the end of the loop stays below the initial noise (the flat fixed point is attracting);
- **PAIR** — the content vector runs into a matching: at most 6 pairs carry at least 95% of the content;
- **INTERMEDIATE** — none of the above three, and the loop stopped within the fixed tolerance.

**The verdict of the package:**

- **The lemma stands** if the INTERMEDIATE class appears **on all six family members and both normalizations**, at least from the start I1. (I2–I4 are a basin question, not a condition of the verdict.)
- **The lemma failed** if from I1 every family member yields FLAT, PAIR or EMPTY. The type of the failure must be named.
- **Partial**, if the family members diverge — in that case **the result is sensitive to the form of the generating rule**, and this is what must be written as the verdict, rather than choosing the favorable member.
- **No verdict comes together** if the loop does not stop within 200 rounds; in that case the fact of cycling is the result, together with the length of the period.

Later extension of the family, new starts, new normalizations and after-the-fact tuning of the tolerances are excluded.

## 8. The readout protocol

**Gate:** the readout runs **only in the case of an INTERMEDIATE verdict.** In the FLAT and PAIR cases there is nothing to read out, and running the readout would count as an after-the-fact search.

In the INTERMEDIATE case, following the order of II/11: the blind rebuilder from the closeness map of the final state, not given the neighbor count — the jump of the list singles that out; a completeness ledger for the found versus the content-bearing pairs, with phantom and missing separately; a ball reading **up to at most $r = 2$**, and **stated: this size is not enough to pronounce an extension number** — it answers only whether there is a distinguished adjacency at all. The extension question is postponed to a larger system.

A resonance sentinel following the lesson of II/13; the antipodal classes reported separately.

## 9. Import ledger and the family requirement

**A new, stated convention — the generating rule:** the content of the contract is computed from the finished state of the instances, through the closeness measure of II/11. This is the single import of this package.

**The family requirement is the price of this import:** following the pattern of the [uniformity theorem](../II-06-universality/proof_en.md), the result stands only if the six family members and the two normalizations give **the same verdict class**. If they do not, the import has not narrowed but has merely moved one level inward — and it must be written that way.

**What this package does not import:** a network (there is none, every pair participates), a budget (there is no quantity to distribute), a field (there is no candidate list), a coordination number (none is stated). **This is the first package on the trail whose inputs do not include a network.**

## 10. Verdict on this package

**Stands.** Its substantive preliminary results: the theorem of the flat fixed point (T1) — which turns the question from existence into stability; the derivation of the ineffectiveness of the empty price on this system (T2), which also settles the package order; and the statement of degeneracy handling as the sensitive point of the loop (T4).

**The B3 precondition ran before the freezing** (`PKG-17-2-verify.py`), and every reference number came back: the bond price exactly 45/66, the degeneracy 132, the closeness spread 1.5·10⁻¹⁶, the bond price of the ring 0.30105 and its closenesses 0.4438 / 0.1231 / 0.0656 / 0.0448 / 0.0366; and in addition the closed form of II/1 and the three numbers of II/5 (3/2, fourfold degeneracy, 15/8). The verification brought **one finding** that modified the text of the rulebook before the freezing: the flat behavior of F5, from which the EMPTY class and the exception to T1 arose.

## 11. Outgoing claims (PKG-17-2 may build only on these)

- **A1:** system = 12 objects, all 66 pairs, a bond contract with variable content; the state is the exact minimum of the full state space, degeneracy with an exact projector (section 3).
- **A2:** the loop according to section 4: six generating family members, two normalizations, four starts, 200 rounds, seed 1166.
- **A3:** the B3 precondition is the reproduction of the control and ring numbers of II/11; the zero test (the noiseless flat start stays in place) is obligatory.
- **A4:** pre-registered facts and questions according to section 6; there is no prediction.
- **A5:** verdict rule according to section 7, with **four classes** (EMPTY, FLAT, PAIR, INTERMEDIATE) and the obligatory writing-out of the partial case; the behavior of F5 must be reported separately in every run.
- **A6:** readout protocol according to section 8, with a gate; no extension verdict may be given at this size.
- **A7:** obligatory self-checks — the closed form of the bond contract by two routes (dense eigenproblem and the formula of II/1), the trace of the projector at the flat point exactly 132, round-by-round reporting of the norm of the content vector, counting of the degeneracy switches.
- **A8:** the failure branch is named: in the case of a **PAIR** verdict the next package repeats the same loop with the **smoothness contract**, on sites, because then the bond contract has proved an unsuitable carrier; in the case of a **FLAT** verdict the loop in this form has failed, and generation must build not on closeness but on some other extract of the state.

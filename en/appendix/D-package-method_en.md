# D) The package trail

The principle of the package method is the language's own methodological rule: [I/1](../I-language/I-01-concept_en.md); its technical mapping (package template, gate rule, branch and PR order) stands in the [rulebook](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). This file keeps the **trail** of the package series that have run: one section per proof, one line per package — what the business of the package was, and what its yield became. The trail of a new proof is always appended to the end as a new section.

## The trail of II/13

[II/13 — the extension race](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/proof_en.md) was built from four packages:

- **[PKG-13-1 — rulebook](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/PKG-13-1-rulebook_en.md):** the candidates, the verdict rule and the readout protocol fixed before every computation; its yield is the coincidence lemma (the four-extension candidate is identical to the plane on 16 sites).
- **[PKG-13-2 — beat ladders](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/PKG-13-2-ladders_en.md):** every ladder by two independent routes; its yield is the discovery of the second segmented weave and the 7 ladder classes.
- **[PKG-13-3 — the filling race](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/PKG-13-3-race_en.md):** the winner table and the double verdict; its yield is the shell logic and the mirror effect.
- **[PKG-13-4 — the readout](../II-proofs/II-13-dimension-race/PKG-13-4-readout_en.md):** closing the loop on the winner; its yield is the exact cause of the partial failure (wrap-around resonance) and the size diagnosis ($+4r$ ball law on the 8×8 weave).

## The trail of II/14

[II/14 — the extension staircase](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/proof_en.md) was built from five packages; *before* the rulebook was fixed a preparatory correction was made (the space ball law of III/2, 8. from linear to quadratic — the protocol builds on this):

- **[PKG-14-1 — rulebook](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/PKG-14-1-rulebook_en.md):** the coordination-six line–plane–space field, the two-band staircase verdict and the ball protocol fixed; its yield is the bipartite mark as a proof of distinctness (the coincidence trap of II/13 excluded) and the ball reading built on the order of growth (constant/linear/quadratic).
- **[PKG-14-2 — beat ladders](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/PKG-14-2-ladders_en.md):** all 224 networks by two independent routes; its yield is the catch of the scan (20 disconnected family members — the correction of a derivation error in the rulebook) and the 25-shelf, exactly symmetric ladder of space.
- **[PKG-14-3 — the filling race](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/PKG-14-3-race_en.md):** the complete staircase verdict (line 2–129, plane 130–255, space 256–511); its yield is the half-filling takeover and the mirror mechanism, together with the field finding (the extension advantage is thin and can be imitated by a line).
- **[PKG-14-4 — the readout](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/PKG-14-4-readout_en.md):** closing the loop on the winner; its yield is the new cause of the partial failure (antipodal mirror echo — the small-size price of the bipartite mark), two rule lessons (a narrow sentinel; the sign gap) and the size diagnosis (an exact $4r^2+2$ on the 12×12×12 weave).
- **[PKG-14-5 — the re-readout](../II-proofs/II-14-dimension-staircase/PKG-14-5-readout-repeat_en.md):** the fixed-scope repetition of the size diagnosis on the 12-weave; its yield: the size artifact goes from candidate to theorem, the echo became a number (16%, signed), and the corrected protocol (signed jump, band sentinel) received a vetted form.

## II/15 — The fourth rung

- **[PKG-15-1 — rulebook](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-1-rulebook_en.md):** the fixed system (20736 sites, coordination eight), the three-rung staircase verdict and the vetted readout protocol; its yield is the principled, three-mark proof of distinctness and the statement of the native-rung shift (8 = 2·4).
- **[PKG-15-2 — beat ladders](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-2-ladders_en.md):** all 503 networks by the closed route, 32 targets by two routes (largest deviation 1.3·10⁻¹²); its yield is two corrections (the J2 row of the walk table of §5; the scope of the even wrap-around) and the clean class finding (502 classes, with no hidden coincidence).
- **[PKG-15-3 — the race](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-3-race_en.md):** the complete band structure (line 2–4217, plane 4218–7964, four extensions 7965–15996, space 15997–20734); its yield is the finding of the top turn, the sharpened confirmation of the mirror rule and the sharpening of the field finding (zero strict victories for the main four).
- **[PKG-15-4 — the readout](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-4-readout_en.md):** closing the loop on the four-extension winner (N = 11075); its yield is the exact cubic ball up to r = 5, the series of harmless echoes (+17.4%) and the two-route seal of 1.7·10⁻¹⁵.
- **[PKG-15-5 — the hole-mirror theorem](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-5-hole-mirror_en.md):** the four-step derivation of the mirror half of the top turn with zero import; its yield: the tie at 20735 and the peak beat at 16 from a theorem, the upper band boundary (15997) as the mirror image of the sparse-end tipping (4740), and the negative control (on a non-bipartite pair the identity does not hold).
- **[PKG-15-6 — the direction theorem](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-6-direction_en.md):** the direction of the sparse stretch raised to a theorem on all six pairs (ordering + inversion + sandwich lemma, with two layers of theorem strength); its yield is the complete pairwise tipping table and the finding of the dominance gap (the beat-by-beat condition holds up to 43–62% of the tipping, independently of the pair).
- **[PKG-15-7 — the top theorem](../II-proofs/II-15-dimension-fourth-rung/PKG-15-7-top_en.md):** threading together the direction theorem and the hole-mirror with no new proof: "at the top three beats four" is a derived fact over the stretch 18649..20734 (strictness margin 0.2072); the measured space band received a two-layer reading.

## II/16 — Coordination ten: the law of the top

- **[PKG-16-1 — rulebook](../II-proofs/II-16-coordination-ten/PKG-16-1-rulebook_en.md):** the selection rule (a stated convention), the four-rung verdict with the named turn-back case, the four-element sparse form of the two-route rule; its yield is the parity finding (the mirror mark belongs to the native alone) and the top question sharpened into a decisive experiment.
- **[PKG-16-2 — beat ladders](../II-proofs/II-16-coordination-ten/PKG-16-2-ladders_en.md):** 805 entrants, streamed, 804 classes; its yield is the catching of the formula error of the residual check by the redundancy of the four-element form (12.8 → 5.3·10⁻¹⁴).
- **[PKG-16-3 — the race](../II-proofs/II-16-coordination-ten/PKG-16-3-race_en.md):** the band structure (line, plane, four extensions, five extensions — three is skipped); its yield is the two named findings and the candidate text of the unified top law.
- **[PKG-16-4 — the readout](../II-proofs/II-16-coordination-ten/PKG-16-4-readout_en.md):** the native winner is five inside and out (an exact fourth-order ball); its yield is the three-element echo series and the vetted sampling projector seal.
- **[PKG-16-5 — theorem transfer](../II-proofs/II-16-coordination-ten/PKG-16-5-transfer_en.md):** the hole-mirror with unchanged text at ten; its yield is the theorem half of the top law on the bipartite set, and the field seal (of the 2000 fillings of the full end, 1999 belong to the bipartite line member).

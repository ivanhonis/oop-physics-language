# Introduction

This document specifies a descriptive language that describes the physical world with object-oriented patterns — not as metaphor: every pattern is sharpened until it yields a computable claim, and that claim is checked against measured quantum-physics results.

The material consists of three parts. **[Part I is the language](I-language/I-00-index_en.md)**: the concepts, the laws, the theorems and the vetted contracts — only what is proven. **[Part II is the proofs](II-proofs/II-00-index_en.md)**: computed results, one by one, analyzed in the same order; every claim of the language receives its evidence here — the complete, current list of the proofs is given by the index of Part II. **[Part III is the frontier](III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)**: the candidate laws and the open questions — what the language does not yet know, stated out loud. A definition refers forward to the proof that establishes it (e.g. "proof: II/1"), so Part I can be read on its own, yet it stays visible what rests on what. The numbering of the proofs is append-only: a new result is always appended to the end of the sequence, and an existing chapter is never renumbered.

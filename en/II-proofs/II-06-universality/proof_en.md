# II/6. The uniformity proof

**The question.** If the smoothness contract is only the price of "being a single object" (a candidate law, [III/1](../../III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)), then its exact form cannot matter: any contract that mixes neighboring sites must give the same lower cost ladder at fine subdivision.

**The computation.** The [box system](../II-03-box/proof_en.md) with four deliberately different local mixing contracts: the proven neighbor smoothness; one that also touches second neighbors; an uneven local one (with random strength site by site); and one decaying with distance. Control: a non-local contract in which every site mixes with every other one equally.

**The result.** All four local mixings give the same lower ladder — **1 : 4 : 9 : 16** (the uneven one within 1–2%, and its deviation disappears as the subdivision is refined). The control gives a completely different ladder: all of its upper levels coincide. Locality is therefore not an empty condition. The verdict has two halves: the *form* of the mixing contract is not an import — kinetic energy is a theorem born inside the language; what remains an import is a single, smaller thing: **locality** itself — that the sites have neighbors, and that mixing runs only between them.

**Checking against reality.** Physics measures this uniformity: the bottom of the bands of crystals always has the same shape, independently of material and lattice — the difference is only scale (effective mass) — and the 1 : 4 : 9 ladder of quantum wells is likewise material-independent. (The physical name of the phenomenon: universality.)

**Import ledger:** the adjacency import of II/3 has narrowed to **locality**; there is no new import. **What it confirmed:** the uniformity theorem; half of the candidate "single object" law ([III/1](../../III-frontier/III-01-candidate-laws_en.md)).
